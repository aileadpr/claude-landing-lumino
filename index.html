FAQ

Is it good for gaming?

Yes. The 2.4GHz wireless connection is designed for low-latency gaming, the linear switches are smooth and fast, and full anti-ghosting (NKRO) registers every key you press at once.

Does it work with Mac?

Yes. It works with Windows and macOS, wired, wireless or via Bluetooth with laptops and tablets.

How long does the battery last?

The 4000mAh battery lasts for weeks of normal use with the backlight off, and several days with RGB on. It recharges via USB-C, and you can keep typing while it charges.

What does "hot-swappable" mean?

You can pull out any switch with the included tool and put in a different one, no soldering needed. Perfect if you want to try new switches later.

Is it loud?

No. The gasket mount and foam layers dampen the noise, giving a deep, soft sound instead of a loud clack. Linear switches have no click.

How long does delivery take?

What if I don't like it?
